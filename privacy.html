Privacy Policy

Pullfolio · Last updated October 19, 2026

Pullfolio is a trading card scanner and collection tracker. It is built to work without an account, and your collection stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your collection, purchase prices, notes and price alerts are stored only on your iPhone. We do not receive them.
  • Camera and photos. Scanning and the centering check run entirely on your device. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. The app only uses photos you pick yourself and does not ask for access to your photo library.

What the app downloads

  • Card data and prices come from our data feed (hosted on Cloudflare Pages). Like any website, the host receives your IP address and standard request information to deliver the files. We do not combine this with anything else and we do not use it to identify you.
  • Card images are loaded from TCGplayer’s image servers, which receive your IP address in the same way. Tapping “View on TCGplayer” opens TCGplayer’s website, which has its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from Apple; it does not receive your name or email address. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Notifications

Price alerts are local notifications created on your device. No push server is involved.

Children

Pullfolio is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can also remove individual cards and alerts inside the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: mazellangames@gmail.com.

Pullfolio is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by any card game publisher or by TCGplayer.
