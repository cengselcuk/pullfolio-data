Privacy Policy

Pullpocket · Last updated November 27, 2026

Pullpocket is a trading card collection tracker: it scans cards, logs pack openings, tracks values and lays out sets as binder pages. It is built to work without an account, and everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your collection and its tags, sealed products, sold cards, wishlist, pack log, purchase and sale prices, notes, price alerts and settings (such as the currency you choose) are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Deck lists you paste are checked on your device and are not sent anywhere.
  • Camera and photos. Scanning and the centering check run entirely on your device. Photos are processed on your device and never uploaded. On Android, text recognition uses Google ML Kit’s on-device model. Your photos and the text read from them stay on the device; ML Kit may send Google anonymous diagnostic data (device and app information, a random per-installation identifier, performance and usage of the feature) to keep it working. The app only uses photos you pick yourself and does not ask for access to your photo library.
  • Backups. “Save a backup” writes everything listed above to a file on your device and opens the share sheet. Where the file goes (your files, a cloud drive, a message) is your choice; we never receive it. “Restore from a backup” only reads the file you pick.
  • CSV files, shared images and lists. Exporting your collection as CSV, sharing a collection or pack-opening image, or sharing a card list as text (such as missing cards, extras or a tag) creates it on your device and opens the share sheet; we never receive it. Importing a CSV file only reads the file you pick, on your device.

What the app downloads

  • Card data, prices and currency exchange rates come from our data feed, hosted on GitHub Pages with Cloudflare Pages as a backup. Like any website, the host receives your IP address and standard request information to deliver the files. We do not combine this with anything else and we do not use it to identify you.
  • Card images are loaded from TCGplayer’s image servers, which receive your IP address in the same way. Tapping “View on TCGplayer” opens TCGplayer’s website, which has its own privacy policy.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store; it does not receive your name or email address. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no analytics of your own use of the app, no advertising, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.

Notifications

Price alerts and the weekly collection summary are local notifications created on your device. No push server is involved. The weekly summary can be turned off in Settings.

Children

Pullpocket is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything it stored. You can also remove individual cards, tags, sealed products, sales, wishlist entries, pack openings and alerts inside the app.
